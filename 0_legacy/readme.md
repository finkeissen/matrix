# matrix

**matrix** is a bounded framework for admissible reasoning under uncertainty.

It does not produce truths, solutions, or world models.
It structures how claims, assumptions, and decisions may be handled
under explicit constraints — and when reasoning must stop.

---

## What matrix is

matrix provides:

- a formal structure for handling epistemic claims under uncertainty
- explicit admissibility criteria for reasoning and decision support
- verification and STOP mechanisms to prevent illegitimate continuation
- a clear separation between diagnostic structure and domain content

matrix operates on *claims about the world*, not on the world itself.

---

## What matrix is not

matrix is **not**:

- a theory of truth
- a worldview or ideology
- a normative or moral system
- a solution generator
- a predictive or explanatory model
- a substitute for judgment, responsibility, or expertise

matrix does not decide *what is true*.
It constrains *what may be reasoned about responsibly*.

---

## Core principles

- **Admissibility over truth**  
  Claims are evaluated by admissibility conditions, not by asserted correctness.

- **Explicit STOP**  
  Reasoning must terminate when continuation becomes inadmissible.

- **No hidden normativity**  
  Normative content must be explicit or is rejected.

- **No authority transfer**  
  Status, consensus, or institutional power do not substitute for admissibility.

- **Bounded scope**  
  No automatic transfer between descriptive, normative, model, or world levels.

## Repository structure

- `1.system/`  
  Canonical specifications, rules, and constraints.

- `2.runs/`  
  Append-only runs applying the framework to concrete cases.

- `foundation/`  
  Non-normative draft material (not part of verification).

- `VERIFICATION_STATUS.md`  
  Current verification state of the framework.

- `VERIFICATION_INDEX.md`  
  Navigation entry point for verification artifacts.

- `PUBLIC_SCOPE.md`  
  Explicit public boundary and misuse constraints.

## Legacy and historical material

Historical, pre-admissibility material has been **intentionally removed**
from this repository.

It is archived separately for documentation purposes only:

→ **https://github.com/finkeissen/legacy**

That archive documents *why certain lines of thought became inadmissible*.
It is non-canonical, non-operative, and not part of matrix verification.

matrix does not depend on its historical antecedents.

---

## Status

matrix is a framework under verification.
It is intentionally minimal, constrained, and incomplete.

Expansion occurs only when forced by admissible application.
