# run_11_politics_problem_seed

Seeds the Matrix with political problems to test:
- normativity and value conflict
- institutional interpretation and legitimacy
- power dynamics and representation
- uncertainty without resolution or advocacy

## Non-goals
- policy advocacy
- normative ranking of positions
- recommendations or prescriptions

## Files
- manifest.json
- problems.jsonl (120 problems)
- claims.jsonl (empty by design)
- relations.jsonl (empty by design)
- sources.jsonl (empty/minimal by design)
