# run_10_economics_problem_seed

Seeds the Matrix with economics problems to test:
- model assumptions and equilibrium concepts
- empirical identification and measurement limits
- tension between theory, data, and policy narratives
- normative overlays without recommendation

## Non-goals
- policy prescriptions
- forecasts
- welfare optimization or ranking

## Files
- manifest.json
- problems.jsonl (120 problems)
- claims.jsonl (empty by design)
- relations.jsonl (empty by design)
- sources.jsonl (empty/minimal by design)
