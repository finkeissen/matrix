# physics

This domain represents **epistemic artifacts related to physics**.

It is a **structured stress environment**
for claims, models, measurements, and interpretations
that are commonly grouped under “physics”.

This domain does **not** represent
physical reality, natural law, or truth.
It represents **what is claimed, by whom, when,
under which assumptions, and in conflict with what**.

---

## Scope of the Physics Domain

This domain may include artifacts related to:

- physical theories and models
- experimental results and measurements
- mathematical formalisms used in physics
- interpretations and frameworks
- methodological assumptions
- historical and contemporary disputes

Inclusion in this domain
does not imply acceptance, correctness, or relevance.

---

## What This Domain Is Not

This domain is **not**:

- a textbook
- a curriculum
- a consensus representation
- a collection of established facts
- a hierarchy of theories

No theory, model, or framework
has privileged status by default.

---

## Coexistence and Conflict

The physics domain explicitly permits:

- parallel theories
- incompatible models
- conflicting interpretations
- unresolved measurement disputes
- historical and contemporary claims side by side

Conflict is expected.
Resolution is not required.

---

## Scales, Contexts, and Fragmentation

Physics operates across many scales and contexts.

This domain does not assume:
- unification
- reduction
- hierarchical ordering
- explanatory completeness

Fragmentation is treated
as an epistemic condition,
not a defect.

---

## Relationship to Other Domains

The physics domain may:

- overlap with other domains (e.g. technology, philosophy, mathematics)
- be referenced by other domains
- reference non-physics domains

Domain boundaries are **pragmatic, not ontological**.

---

## Relationship to Runs

No content appears in this domain
without at least one corresponding run.

Runs document:
- how artifacts entered the domain
- which constraints applied
- which conflicts were surfaced

Unrun content belongs in `work/`.

---

## Known Open Questions (Indicative)

This list is intentionally incomplete.

Examples include:
- limits of current physical models
- interpretation disputes
- unresolved experimental tensions
- scale-bridging problems

These questions are represented,
not answered.

---

## Summary

> **This domain does not describe the world.
> It describes how the world is described in physics.**

It exists to expose structure under uncertainty,
not to collapse it into knowledge.
