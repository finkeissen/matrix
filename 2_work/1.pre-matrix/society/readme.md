# society

This domain represents **epistemic artifacts related to society**.

It is a structured stress environment for:
- claims about social structures and relations
- institutions and collective behavior
- norms, roles, and social dynamics
- inequality, power, and stratification
- empirical and theoretical social analysis

This domain does **not** represent social reality itself.
It represents **what is claimed about society,
by whom, when,
under which assumptions,
and in conflict with what**.

---

## Why Society Is Treated as a Primary Domain

Society is not an epistemic regime,
but a **complex object of description**
addressed by many regimes simultaneously.

It is characterized by:
- high complexity and reflexivity
- interaction between description and behavior
- entanglement of empirical and normative claims
- dependence on perspective and scale

These properties make society
structurally difficult and epistemically unstable.

---

## Scope of This Domain

This domain may include artifacts related to:

- social structures and institutions
- demographic and social data (as claims)
- theories of social interaction
- inequality and power relations
- collective action and movements
- critiques and counter-claims

Inclusion does not imply correctness or consensus.

---

## What This Domain Is Not

This domain is **not**:

- a policy framework
- a sociological canon
- a consensus model
- a guide for intervention
- a replacement for political judgment

No theory or method is privileged by default.

---

## Conflict and Multiplicity

The society domain explicitly permits:

- competing theories
- conflicting empirical interpretations
- divergent normative perspectives
- incompatible scales of analysis

Conflict is expected.
Resolution is not required.

---

## Relationship to Other Domains

Society intersects with:
- politics and governance
- economics and production
- culture and identity
- psychology and behavior
- technology and infrastructure

Cross-domain conflict is expected and permitted.

---

## Relationship to Runs

No content appears in this domain
without at least one corresponding run.

Runs document:
- inputs
- constraints
- produced artifacts
- surfaced conflicts and gaps

Unrun content belongs in `work/`.

---

## Known Open Questions (Indicative)

This list is intentionally incomplete.

Examples include:
- limits of social measurement
- interaction between structure and agency
- reflexivity of social theories
- persistence of inequality

These questions are represented, not answered.

---

## Summary

> **This domain does not explain society.
> It explains how society is explained.**

It exists to expose structure,
assumptions,
and conflict under social complexity.
