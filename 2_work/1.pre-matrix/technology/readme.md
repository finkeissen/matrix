# technology

This domain represents **epistemic artifacts related to technology**.

It is a structured stress environment for:
- claims about technological systems and artifacts
- design choices and constraints
- use, impact, and failure
- interaction between technology and society
- narratives of progress, risk, and control

This domain does **not** represent technological correctness
or prescribe technological choices.
It represents **what is claimed, by whom, when,
under which assumptions, and in conflict with what**.

---

## Why Technology Is Treated as a Primary Domain

Technology is not just applied science.

It is characterized by:
- material constraints and implementation realities
- embedded assumptions and values
- unintended consequences and failure modes
- rapid change and obsolescence
- deep entanglement with power and governance

These properties make technology
epistemically complex and practically consequential.

---

## Scope of This Domain

This domain may include artifacts related to:

- technological systems and infrastructures
- engineering claims and design rationales
- performance and reliability claims
- risk, safety, and failure analysis
- social and political impacts of technology
- critiques and alternative designs

Inclusion does not imply endorsement or safety.

---

## What This Domain Is Not

This domain is **not**:

- a best-practice manual
- a standards body
- a technology roadmap
- a certification authority
- a decision framework for adoption

No technology or design is privileged by default.

---

## Conflict and Consequence

The technology domain explicitly permits:

- competing design philosophies
- conflicting performance claims
- disputes over risk and benefit
- tension between innovation and control

Consequences may be severe,
but resolution is not required here.

---

## Relationship to Other Domains

Technology intersects with:
- informatics and computation
- physics and material constraints
- economics and production
- politics and governance
- culture and everyday practice

Cross-domain conflict is expected and permitted.

---

## Relationship to Runs

No content appears in this domain
without at least one corresponding run.

Runs document:
- inputs
- constraints
- produced artifacts
- surfaced conflicts and gaps

Unrun content belongs in `work/`.

---

## Known Open Questions (Indicative)

This list is intentionally incomplete.

Examples include:
- predictability of technological impact
- limits of control and governance
- interaction between design and use
- long-term consequences of deployment

These questions are represented, not answered.

---

## Summary

> **This domain does not validate technology.
> It describes how technology is described, built, and contested.**

It exists to expose structure,
assumptions,
and conflict under material constraint.
