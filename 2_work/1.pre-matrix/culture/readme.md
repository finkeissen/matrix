# culture

This domain represents **epistemic artifacts related to culture**.

It is a structured stress environment for:
- claims about meaning, symbols, practices, and expression
- interpretations of art, media, rituals, and norms
- cultural frameworks and traditions
- conflicts over values, identity, and representation
- historical and contemporary cultural analysis

This domain does **not** represent cultural truth or authority.
It represents **what is claimed, interpreted, contested,
by whom, when,
and in conflict with what**.

---

## Why Culture Is Treated as a Primary Domain

Culture is not a single epistemic regime.

It is a **convergence space** where:
- multiple knowledge systems interact
- interpretation is unavoidable
- normativity is often implicit
- meaning is context-dependent
- boundaries between description and evaluation blur

These properties make culture
structurally unstable and epistemically sensitive.

---

## Scope of This Domain

This domain may include artifacts related to:

- art and aesthetic interpretation
- literature and media
- rituals, symbols, and practices
- identity formation and expression
- cultural narratives and myths
- critiques and counter-interpretations

Inclusion does not imply endorsement or representativeness.

---

## What This Domain Is Not

This domain is **not**:

- a canon
- a value system
- a cultural hierarchy
- a normative framework
- a representation of “shared meaning”

No tradition, identity, or interpretation
is privileged by default.

---

## Conflict and Normativity

The culture domain explicitly permits:

- conflicting interpretations
- incompatible value frameworks
- disputes over meaning and representation
- parallel narratives and counter-narratives

Normativity must be explicit.
Implicit normativity is treated as structural failure.

---

## Relationship to Other Domains

Culture intersects with:
- history and memory
- linguistics and communication
- politics and power
- psychology and identity
- technology and media

These intersections are representable,
but never silently collapsed.

---

## Relationship to Runs

No content appears in this domain
without at least one corresponding run.

Runs document:
- inputs
- constraints
- produced artifacts
- surfaced conflicts and gaps

Unrun content belongs in `work/`.

---

## Known Open Questions (Indicative)

This list is intentionally incomplete.

Examples include:
- limits of interpretation
- relationship between culture and power
- role of media and technology
- persistence and change of cultural forms

These questions are represented, not answered.

---

## Summary

> **This domain does not define culture.
> It describes how culture is described and contested.**

It exists to preserve plurality,
conflict,
and interpretive limits.
