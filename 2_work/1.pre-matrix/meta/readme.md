# meta

This domain contains **epistemic artifacts about the Matrix itself**.

It is a **self-referential stress environment**:
the Matrix applies its own structural principles
to its own assumptions, limits, and behavior.

This domain has **no privileged status**.
It is subject to the same constraints,
the same uncertainty,
and the same revision rules
as any other domain.

---

## Purpose of the Meta Domain

The meta domain exists to represent:

- assumptions embedded in the Matrix architecture
- unresolved design tensions
- documented limitations
- internal conflicts
- self-applied STOP conditions

It exists to prevent
the Matrix from becoming
an unexamined background authority.

---

## What Belongs in This Domain

This domain may contain artifacts such as:

- claims about Matrix design choices
- alternative architectural interpretations
- conflicts between design goals
- gaps where design questions remain unresolved
- historical shifts in assumptions

All such artifacts are treated
as **claims**, not as ground truth.

---

## What Does Not Belong in This Domain

This domain must not contain:

- normative system rules (those belong in `system/`)
- explanatory material (those belong in `handbook/`)
- operational logic (those belong in MMS)
- authority claims about other domains

Meta is **reflective**, not controlling.

---

## Relationship to Other Domains

The meta domain:

- may reference other domains
- may be referenced by other domains
- does not override any domain
- does not arbitrate conflicts

Cross-domain conflict involving meta
is expected and permitted.

---

## Relationship to Runs

Artifacts in this domain
enter and change only via runs.

Design discussions without runs
belong in `work/`, not here.

---

## Known Open Questions (Non-Exhaustive)

This list is intentionally incomplete.

Examples may include:
- how much heterogeneity the Matrix can sustain
- when schemas become constraining
- how self-reference scales with size
- where explicit STOP conditions should apply

These questions are represented,
not answered.

---

## Summary

> **If the Matrix cannot represent uncertainty about itself,
> it cannot represent uncertainty about anything else.**

This domain exists
to keep that test permanently active.
